BigEarthNet.txt (the mandatory adaptation dataset) is hosted at txt.bigearth.net, built from co-registered Sentinel-1/Sentinel-2 pairs. VRSBench (captioning/grounding/VQA) is at vrsbench.github.io / github.com/lx709/VRSBench. RSVQA (Lobry et al.) is at rsvqa.sylvainlobry.com and github.com/syvlo/RSVQA, with the BigEarthNet-crossed variant at github.com/syvlo/RSVQAxBEN. CDVQA is at github.com/YZHJessica/CDVQA, built on the public SECOND change-detection dataset. None of these need permission — all are open academic releases. The one I'd add beyond the problem statement's list: Bhoonidhi (bhoonidhi.nrsc.gov.in), ISRO's own free/open data hub, which actually carries RISAT and Cartosat-3 products plus a regional Sentinel mirror. Pulling a handful of real Indian-sensor pairs from there early lets you sanity-check your cross-modal module against something closer to the hidden ISRO/SAC evaluation set, instead of finding out about the Sentinel→Cartosat/RISAT domain gap for the first time during judging.
